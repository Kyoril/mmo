# Acolyte spell presentation

Date: 2026-10-16. Status: particles, sounds, sprites and visualization kits installed; in-game
acceptance of timing and look still required (see "Verification").

Follows the warrior, cleric, mage and scout passes ([warrior_spell_visuals.md](warrior_spell_visuals.md),
[cleric_spell_visuals.md](cleric_spell_visuals.md), [mage_spell_visuals.md](mage_spell_visuals.md),
[scout_spell_visuals.md](scout_spell_visuals.md)) and keeps their rules: no kit sets
`duration_ms`, kits use `sound_ids` (catalog entries), no sound on aura events, aura kits are
TARGET-scoped, stylized particles built from density at low alpha because the renderer has no
additive blending and no bloom.

## What was there before

Class 3 (Acolyte, classmask 4) has four trained spells and four talent-built ones that show
anything. Before this pass:

- Shadowbolt (23, 74) shared the pre-catalog "Shadow Bolt" entry (16) with creature spells: a
  `CastLoop` with a raw shadow loop, a cast sound, and a sphere *mesh* projectile with a ribbon.
  No impact at all.
- Corrupting Touch (24) used the default visualization (9): `CastLoop` / `CastRelease`, nothing
  else -- no sound, no particle, nothing on the victim for 12 s of damage.
- Fear (73) had a loop sound and an impact sound, no particles.
- Veil of Shadows (38) was a `CastRelease` with a hand light and a raw sound on IMPACT; Embrace of
  Dread (1328), the Dread capstone, borrowed it.
- Umbral Bloom (1309), the Umbra capstone, borrowed the mage's "Arcane Pulse" (55): pink-arcane
  rings on a shadow caster.
- Gloaming Trance (1306), Leeching Rot (1314) and Void Resurgence (119-121) showed nothing.

## Art direction

**Whispering void, wet rot, cold dread.** The acolyte is the one class whose magic should make
the viewer uneasy. Every effect pairs a **dark body** (charcoal-violet smoke) with a
**saturated rim** of its school colour: darkness cannot glow in an alpha-only renderer, so an
all-black effect vanishes on a dark floor and reads as dirt on a bright one.

| School | Particles | Audio |
|---|---|---|
| Shadow (Shadowbolt, Veil, Umbral Bloom, Gloaming, Void Resurgence) | violet rim on charcoal smoke, curling wisps that spiral *inward* while casting, lilac stars | low swirling hum, wordless whispers, hollow glassy cracks, sub drops |
| Blight (Corrupting Touch, Leeching Rot) | acid-green droplets falling under gravity, swelling bubbles, olive-black miasma, a faint leering skull | wet slimy squelch, acid hiss, bubbling channel loop |
| Dread (Fear, Embrace of Dread) | crimson on black, skulls, the occult sigil burning onto the ground | reversed swell, wordless spectral wail, a cracked funeral bell |

Three new sprites, generated by `tools/particle_gen/make_sprites.py` as DXT5 `.htex` plus `.hmi`
instances of `Particle_Alpha_Tex.hmat` (translucent, depth-write off):

- `Particle_Skull` -- a scowling skull, eye sockets and nose cut out of the alpha so a tinted skull
  reads as a spectral face with hollow eyes. Never rolled.
- `Particle_Wisp` -- a soft head trailing an S-curved tail: rolled billboards read as restless
  spirits curling off a body.
- `Particle_VoidSigil` -- a broken occult circle with a heptagram and inward thorns, the dark
  counterpart of the cleric's holy `Particle_Sigil`.

Recipes: `tools/particle_gen/recipes/acolyte_{common,shadow,blight_dread}.py`, output in
`data/client/Particles/Acolyte/` (21 effects). Sounds: `tools/sfx_gen/recipes/acolyte.py`, output
in `data/client/Sound/Spells/Acolyte/`.

### Timing

Fear's cast sound is a reversed swell that peaks at 0.98 s. It sits in the CASTING event as a
non-looping kit delayed 1000 ms, so on the base 2.0 s cast it ends on the release. With Dread
Presence (cast 1.0 s) the delayed kit is dropped at cast end, which is the service's existing
behaviour for pending cast-phase kits, and only the channel loop plays. Embrace of Dread is
instant, so its sound starts on the bell toll rather than a swell (a swell would put the hit
0.9 s after the burst).

## Sounds

16 layers, each generated on its own (one element per prompt, music excluded, voice excluded or
the whisper/wail layers kept wordless), four takes each, preselected on measurements (peak
position, length, spectral centroid, low-band share), mixed by `tools/sfx_gen/layer.py` per the
recipe's `MIXES` into 11 one-shot finals; the two channel loops are single loop-mode
generations (`SINGLES`). Rejected on measurement: `Void_Burst` take 3 (peak at 99% of the clip);
`Shadow_Crack` 2/4 and `Dark_Chime` 2 came back half-length. Render all finals with
`py tools/sfx_gen/recipes/acolyte.py --render generated/acolyte_visuals/sfx/layers`; the audition
reel is `generated/acolyte_visuals/Acolyte_Audition.wav`.

  the skull sizes over heads, and whether the dark bodies read on both bright and dark ground.
