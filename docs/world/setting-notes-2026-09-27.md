Provided by the user as the "Established" source layer for `docs/world/bible.md`

## The world's identity
Medieval high fantasy MMORPG with a grounded, lived-in feel. The opening human region is a forest with
camps, farms, roads, a village and a fortified town. Local troubles: dangerous wildlife, banditry,
kobolds. Guards, farmers, healers and class trainers give places a practical everyday life.
Visual direction: stylized, colorful, hand-painted (not realistic); more variation in forest colors
and textures wanted so locations feel distinct. Inspired by the sense of place and adventure of
classic MMORPGs (esp. WoW Classic); Alestia's wider history and mythology are not yet defined.

Possible expansion outline (not established): Heartlands → Northern Mountains → Far Coast → a foreign
kingdom → an unknown continent.

## People, communities, threats
Ordinary civilian and martial roles: farmers, healers, guards, vendors, trainers, an armsmaster.
Haven supports specialist training and trade; Oakenshire and the forest camp are smaller, more
vulnerable communities.
- Forest boars incl. Young Forest Boars — may be neutral until attacked or made relevant by a task.
- Bandits — operate near Oakenshire, can ambush travellers on the western approach.
- Kobolds — established creature group for the region; society and role undefined.
Haven/Oakenshire guards and militia, Falwyn defenders, Westroad labourers appeared in drafts; their
organizations, ranks and loyalties are not fixed. "Crown" / "Light" in suggested dialogue were
possible expressions, not confirmation of a monarchy or religion.

## Opening stories and quests
1. *The Boar Problem* — Haldor: kill ten Young Forest Boars, report back.
2. *Gathering Healing Herbs* — Mirenna: five Silverleaf Sprigs found near large trees; unlocks her
   minor potions. Silverleaf is an established medicinal plant.
3. *A Crate Too Far* — bandits west of Oakenshire ambush someone and steal a crate; player returns it.
4. *An Urgent Request* — Emrik gives a warrior a letter from Baldric Steelheart; meet him in Haven.
5. *The Warrior's Trial → Lessons in Steel* — ~level 10, Armsmaster Theobald Kerrin; three-wave arena
   ending with Sergeant Bram Halford; Theobald calls out at start and after victory. Reward intended
   as a desirable uncommon sword ("Blade of the First Watch" is provisional).
A later cleric healing quest with a guard in Haven (~level 5) was discussed; details not recorded.

**Firm rule:** the player must return to an NPC or object to turn in every quest; gathering/killing
never completes a quest automatically.

## Classes in the fiction
Warrior, Mage, Cleric, Scout, Shadowmancer. Haven trains Warrior, Mage, Cleric, Shadowmancer; Scout
training location not established.
- Warrior: martial training, weapons, formal trial of skill in Haven.
- Mage: spellcasting incl. early frost magic.
- Cleric: dedicated healer and holy-style caster, not a melee fighter.
- Scout: agile close-combat with daggers or one-handed swords.
- Shadowmancer: shadow and DoT magic; power/recovery tied to defeated enemies.
Source, institutions, costs, religious meaning of magic are undefined; "Cleric" names no god/church.

## Travel and scale
The world should feel huge; journeys through the landscape keep their meaning. A faster transport
system without personal mounts is being considered. Roads, passes, mine carts, carts/wagons and
ships have come up; mine cart concept art exists. No route network, operators, fares or fast-travel
rules established.

## Objects and motifs
Silverleaf Sprigs and minor potions (local herbal practice); letters between camp and Haven;
crates, roads, labourers, carts, mine carts (trade and transport matter); Haven Guard gear, Westwatch
armour names, Blade of the First Watch (traditions undefined); planned Field Journal / Bestiary
(zone-organized entries for selected creatures; in-world object vs institution vs UI open).
