# Scout spell presentation

Date: 2026-10-12. Status: particles, sounds, visualization kits and one engine extension
installed; in-game acceptance of timing and look still required (see "Verification").

Follows the warrior, cleric and mage passes ([warrior_spell_visuals.md](warrior_spell_visuals.md),
[cleric_spell_visuals.md](cleric_spell_visuals.md), [mage_spell_visuals.md](mage_spell_visuals.md))
and keeps their rules: no kit sets `duration_ms`, kits use `sound_ids` (catalog entries), no
sound on aura events, aura kits are TARGET-scoped, stylized particles built from density at low
alpha because the renderer has no additive blending and no bloom.

## What was there before

Class 4 (Scout) has 13 active abilities across 14 spell entries (two Wounding Strike ranks) plus
the Wounded bleed (165) they apply. Before this pass:

- Sprint, Knockout, Quick Cut, Evasive Step, Steady Hand and Smoke and Mirrors had no
  visualization at all. Throwing Knife had none either; entry 20 "Throwing Knife" existed but was
  empty and unreferenced.
- Wounding Strike (18) and Ruthless Strike (19) were an attack animation each, plus a `Hit`
  target animation on CAST_SUCCEEDED that could never run (no target list on that event, and no
  rig has a `Hit` clip). Twin Slash and Mark Weakness borrowed those two entries.
- Fever Pitch borrowed the warrior's legacy "Bloodrush" (4): an empty sound path and a red tint.
- Wounded pointed at "Deep Wound" (6), an empty shell.
- No particle and no sound anywhere.

## Art direction

**Quick, precise, dirty.** A scout attacks every second, so every effect is short and sharp and
nothing lingers or fills the screen.

| Element | Particles | Audio |
|---|---|---|
| Blades | cold silver blade arcs (ribbons) on the weapon hand, crescent cut sprites, star glints, steel sparks | thin fast swishes, wet cuts, a bright steel ring |
| Wounds | vivid stylized crimson droplets and a soft blood puff | wet spatter, a short dark "curse" sting |
| Evasion | charcoal-violet smoke, shadow streaks, silver "mirror" glints | cloak and shadow whooshes, smoke-bomb pop and hiss, glassy shimmer |
| Energy | amber streaks and crackling stars (the energy bar's colour) | heartbeat and a rising electric surge |
| Stun / mark | gold "seeing stars" over the head; a crimson crosshair over the marked target | pommel knock + thump + dizzy chimes; a low plucked sting and a cocking click |

Three new sprites, generated by `tools/particle_gen/make_sprites.py` as DXT5 `.htex` plus `.hmi`
instances of `Particle_Alpha_Tex.hmat` (translucent, depth-write off):

- `Particle_Slash` -- a crescent whose middle passes through the quad centre, so a random roll
  keeps every cut on the target and two cuts with different rolls cross at the impact (Twin
  Slash's X).
- `Particle_Smoke` -- a lumpy soft puff; round glow blobs read as light, not smoke.
- `Particle_Crosshair` -- the hunter's mark reticle.

Recipes: `tools/particle_gen/recipes/scout_{common,strikes,utility}.py`, output in
`data/client/Particles/Scout/` (23 effects). Sounds: `tools/sfx_gen/recipes/scout.py`, output in
`data/client/Sound/Spells/Scout/`.

### Timing

Instant melee IMPACT is raised at SpellGo -- when the swing *starts* -- so impacts are delayed to
where the blade connects: 280 ms for the 1H attack clips (0.97 s), 180 ms for `UnarmedAttack01`
(0.70 s male / 0.83 s female). The strike sounds carry the same structure inside the mix: the
whoosh starts at 0 and the cut lands 0.12-0.15 s in. Blade arcs start 60-120 ms into the clip and
emit for 0.3-0.45 s. All of these are feel values from clip lengths, not from contact frames;
they are the first thing to tune once the effects are seen in game.

## Engine change: self-retiring ribbon trails

Blade arcs are the scout's signature, and a ribbon on a one-shot kit could not exist before:
`SpellVisualizationService` only tears ribbons down at cast end (cast-phase effects) or aura
removal, so a ribbon spawned by CAST_SUCCEEDED of a spell without an aura would follow the hand
for the rest of the session. The cleric ribbons are safe only because they sit on CASTING kits.

`RibbonTrailConfig` gains two fields (both proto copies, same numbers):

- `emit_duration = 14` -- seconds the trail adds segments. 0 (default) keeps the old behaviour.
  With a value, the trail stops emitting after that time and is destroyed once its last segment
  has faded (`spell_visual::TimedRibbonPhase`, unit-tested in `game_client_tests`), so its arc
  fades instead of popping.
- `segment_interval = 15` -- seconds between segments (default 0.05, the old hard-coded value). A
  weapon swing needs ~0.012 s or the arc is a visible polyline.

Wired in the client service, the projectile manager (interval only), and the editor's
visualization preview and kit UI. Visualization contents never cross the wire: no
`ProtocolVersion` bump.

Ribbons are given an explicit material (`Particles/Particle_Beam.hmi`). The default ribbon
materials (`Materials/RibbonTrail_Additive.hmat` and its fallback) do not exist in the data, so a
ribbon without a material has nothing to render with. Ribbon UVs run `u` along the trail and `v`
across it; the beam sprite's tapered ends therefore give the ribbon soft edges, and its bright
middle makes each arc read as a crescent.

## Sounds

21 layers, each generated on its own (one element per prompt, voice and music excluded), three
takes each, preselected on measurements (peak position, attack, spectral centroid, low-band
share), mixed by `tools/sfx_gen/layer.py` per the recipe's `MIXES` into 14 finals. Layers are
shared between finals where the element is the same, so the techniques sound like one fighter.
Rejected on measurement: `Smoke_Pop` takes 1-2 and `Slice_Flesh` take 3 (peak past half the
clip). Render all finals with
`py tools/sfx_gen/recipes/scout.py --render generated/scout_visuals/sfx/layers`; the audition reel
is `generated/scout_visuals/Scout_Audition.wav`.

  visualization, every ribbon timed, every particle validated) and `ScoutRecipeTests` in

## Remaining work

- In-game acceptance of the blade arcs and impact timing on both human rigs; the 280/180 ms
  delays and the arc windows are guesses from clip lengths.
- Twin Slash's second target gets no impact: instant IMPACT uses the explicit target only (the
  same confirmed-hit-list limitation as Cleave).
- `Throwing Knife` uses a particle "knife" (spinning glint + stretched blade streak). A real knife
  mesh on the projectile (`ProjectileVisual.mesh_name` works in the client) would need a suitable
  model; `SM_hc_Knife` is a kitchen prop.
- Orc rigs lack `Attack_1H_*` and `CastRelease`; the service skips the animation and still plays
  particles, ribbons and sound.
- Stealth (234, "Stealth Test") is not granted to the class and was left alone.
