auto-retried by the service did **not** hold here: 7 of the 28 first-attempt generations
(all in the second run_flow_nodes call, the last 8 female clips) came back with
`status: "failed"` and error message `"Too many concurrent requests... maximum of 5
concurrent requests"`. Re-polling the same session_ids repeatedly left them `failed`
(the service does not silently retry a `failed` generation). These were explicitly re-run
via `creative_run_flow_nodes`:

- First retry batch (7 nodes together): 5 succeeded, 2 still failed with the same error
  (`HumanFemale_Pain_02` "Aah!", `HumanFemale_PainCrit_03` "Hnnngh!").
- Final retries (1 node at a time, to stay under the 5-concurrent cap): both succeeded.

Total generation calls: 28 (first attempt) + 7 (retry batch) + 2 (final single retries) =
37, under the 40-call stop-and-report threshold, so no need to halt.
